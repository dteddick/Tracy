FAQ

Common questions

Do I need W-2s or tax returns to qualify?

Not for DSCR, fix and flip or bank statement loans. DSCR loans qualify on the property's rental income, fix and flip loans qualify on the deal and your experience, and bank statement loans use 12 to 24 months of bank deposits instead of tax returns.

What is a DSCR loan?

A Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan qualifies you based on the property's rental income compared to its monthly payment, rather than your personal income. DSCR loans are available on properties from 1 to 10 units and can close in an LLC.

How many units can a DSCR loan cover?

DSCR loans can finance properties of up to 10 units, from single-family homes and condos to duplexes, fourplexes and small apartment buildings.

Which states do you lend in?

Tracy can originate DSCR and fix and flip loans in most states across the country. Home purchase loans are focused on Central Florida. Call 352-223-0712 to confirm availability for your property's location.

Do you offer ground-up construction loans?

Yes. Ground-up construction loans are available for investors building new homes to sell or rent, with funds released on a draw schedule as construction progresses.

Do I need experience to get a fix and flip loan?

Experience can improve your terms, but many lenders work with first-time flippers. We'll review your deal, rehab budget and team to find a program that fits.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. Most investor programs, including DSCR, fix and flip and construction loans, allow you to close in the name of your LLC or other business entity.

Why work with a mortgage broker?

There is no one-size-fits-all loan. A broker works with many lenders instead of one, which means more programs, more options for your real estate strategy and someone advocating for you.
