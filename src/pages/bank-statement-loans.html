How bank statement loans work

Instead of W-2s and tax returns, the lender reviews 12 to 24 months of your personal or business bank statements to calculate qualifying income from your deposits. It's built for business owners, 1099 contractors, gig workers and real estate professionals.

Who they're for

  • Small business owners and entrepreneurs
  • 1099 contractors and freelancers
  • Real estate agents and investors
  • Anyone whose tax returns understate their true income

Bank statement loan questions

Do bank statement loans require tax returns?

No. Bank statement loans use 12 to 24 months of personal or business bank statements to determine income, so W-2s and tax returns are not required.

Can I use a bank statement loan for my primary home?

Yes. Bank statement loans can be used for a primary residence, second home or investment property.

Who should consider a bank statement loan?

Self-employed borrowers, business owners, 1099 contractors and anyone whose tax returns don't reflect their true income because of business write-offs.
