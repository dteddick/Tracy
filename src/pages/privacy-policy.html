This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and what choices you have regarding your personal information.

What Information You Provide

When you complete a form on our website, Facebook, Instagram, or another advertising platform, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • The type of mortgage or financing you are exploring.
  • Whether you are purchasing, refinancing, or renovating a property.
  • Property location, type, and estimated value.
  • Your estimated financing needs and timeline.
  • Your preferred method of contact.
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide.

If you proceed with a financing application, additional financial and personal information may be requested through an appropriate application process.

You are not required to complete an online inquiry form. You may contact Tracy directly at 352-223-0712 or tracy@mpirefi.com.

What Is Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

  • Pages visited and website activity.
  • Approximate geographic location.
  • Device and browser information.
  • Referral websites and advertising sources.
  • Cookies and similar tracking information.

Our website may use analytics and advertising tools, including Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, to measure website traffic, evaluate advertising performance, and improve marketing.

You can manage certain tracking preferences through your browser settings and the privacy controls available on Facebook, Instagram, and Google.

Why We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to mortgage and financing inquiries.
  • Discuss available home purchase and refinance loan options.
  • Provide information about DSCR, Fix & Flip, and investment property financing.
  • Review potential financing programs based on your needs.
  • Contact you through your preferred communication method.
  • Assist with mortgage applications and related services when requested.
  • Improve our website, advertising, and customer service.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping requirements.

Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee loan approval, financing eligibility, or specific loan terms.

Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

When you submit a lead form through Facebook or Instagram, the information you provide may be received by Tracy Freeman and MPIRE FINANCIAL GROUP, LLC.

We use this information to contact you about the mortgage or investment property financing options you requested.

Your information may also be shared with authorized lending partners or service providers when necessary to evaluate financing options or provide requested services, consistent with applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Text Messages

If you provide your phone number and give the required consent to receive text messages, Tracy the Mortgage Lady or MPIRE FINANCIAL GROUP, LLC may contact you regarding your financing inquiry, appointment, or requested services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from text messages or HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is optional and is not required to apply for financing or obtain services.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent information are not sold or shared with third parties for their independent marketing or promotional purposes.

Email Communications

If you provide your email address, we may use it to respond to your inquiry, provide requested financing information, or follow up regarding services you have expressed interest in.

With appropriate consent where required, we may also send educational updates and information about available financing programs.

Marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option where required. You may request to stop receiving marketing communications at any time.

Who May Receive Your Information

We may share information with parties involved in providing our services, including:

  • Authorized lenders and financing partners when necessary for requested mortgage services.
  • Loan processing, underwriting, and other mortgage service providers.
  • Customer relationship management (CRM), website hosting, and communication service providers.
  • Advertising and analytics providers, where applicable.
  • Government agencies, regulators, or other parties when required by law.

Information is shared only as permitted by applicable law and consistent with our business practices and privacy disclosures.

We do not sell or rent personal contact information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Mortgage application and transaction records may be retained for longer periods where required by law.

When information is no longer needed, it will be deleted or securely disposed of according to applicable retention requirements.
