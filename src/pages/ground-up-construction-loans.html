How ground-up construction loans work

A ground-up construction loan funds the building of a new property. Lenders review your plans, budget, timeline, builder and the projected completed value. Funds are released in draws as each construction milestone is reached and inspected.

When construction is complete, you can sell the property or refinance into a long-term DSCR loan to hold it as a rental.

Good fit for

  • Investors building spec homes to sell
  • Build-to-rent single-family and small multifamily projects
  • Investors with a lot ready to build
  • Experienced flippers moving into new construction

Construction loan questions

Are ground-up construction loans available for investors?

Yes. Ground-up construction loans are available for real estate investors building new homes to sell or to rent.

How do construction draws work?

The loan is funded in stages. After each phase of construction is completed and inspected, the next draw is released to pay for the work.

Can I refinance a new build into a rental loan?

Yes. Once the property is complete and rented, you can refinance into a long-term DSCR loan that qualifies on the rental income.
