What is a DSCR loan?

A Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loan is a mortgage for investment properties that qualifies you based on the property's cash flow. The lender divides the monthly rent by the monthly payment (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA). A ratio of 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Many programs look for 1.0 or higher, and some allow lower.

Because the property qualifies itself, DSCR loans are a favorite of self-employed investors, people who write off most of their income, and anyone scaling past the limits of conventional financing.

Who DSCR loans are for

  • Investors buying or refinancing long-term rentals
  • Short-term and vacation rental owners (select programs)
  • BRRRR investors refinancing out of a fix & flip or bridge loan
  • Small multifamily owners with up to 10 units
  • Out-of-state investors buying in Florida, and Florida investors buying elsewhere

DSCR loan questions

Do DSCR loans require tax returns or W-2s?

No. DSCR loans qualify using the property's rental income compared to its monthly payment, so you don't need to provide W-2s, pay stubs or tax returns.

How many units can a DSCR loan finance?

DSCR loans can finance properties with 1 to 10 units, including single-family homes, condos, duplexes, fourplexes and small apartment buildings.

Can I use a DSCR loan for a short-term rental?

Yes, select DSCR programs can qualify short-term and vacation rentals using short-term rental income. Guidelines vary by lender and market.
