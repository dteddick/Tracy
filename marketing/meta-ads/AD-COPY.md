## Ads Manager setup notes

- **Special Ad Category:** you must turn on *Financial products and services* (formerly "Credit") for any loan ad. If you don't, Meta will reject the ad or restrict the account.
- With that category on, Meta limits targeting: no age, gender or ZIP code targeting, and location targeting needs at least a 15-mile radius. Target by state or city plus interests such as real estate investing, house flipping or BiggerPockets.
- Run the ad from the Facebook Page you use for Tracy the Mortgage Lady.
- Have Mpire compliance approve the image and text before you launch. The 90% / 100% figures, "No W-2s or tax returns" and "most states" claims must match the current program guidelines.
