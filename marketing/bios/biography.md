## From caring for patients to building wealth through real estate

For thirty years, Tracy Freeman's work was about caring for people. As a nurse, she spent her days at the bedside, helping patients and families through some of the most stressful moments of their lives. She learned to listen first, explain clearly and stay calm when others couldn't. Those years shaped who she is, and they still shape every conversation she has with clients today.

## Roots in New York, home in Central Florida

Tracy is originally from New York, and she still carries a New Yorker's work ethic and straight talk. Today she and her husband call Central Florida home. She loves the region's growth, its neighborhoods and its people.

## Becoming an investor

While she was still working as a nurse, Tracy started looking for ways to build long-term financial security for her family. She found real estate.

She began buying properties as long-term buy-and-hold rentals, building steady cash flow and equity over time. As she gained experience, she explored other investment strategies as well. Each deal taught her something new about financing, about numbers and about what it really takes to grow a portfolio.

Along the way, Tracy saw the challenges investors face with lenders. Traditional loans weren't built for people with rental income, self-employment income or several properties. She learned firsthand that the right financing can make a deal possible, and the wrong financing can stop it cold.

## A new calling

In 2021, after three decades in healthcare, Tracy left nursing and became a licensed mortgage broker. It was a natural next step. She already understood real estate as an investor, and she already knew how to guide people through big, unfamiliar decisions as a nurse.

Today Tracy is a Mortgage Loan Originator (NMLS #2174804) with Mpire Financial Group LLC (NMLS #2108504). She works with real estate investors and home buyers, drawing on both her nursing background and her own experience as an investor. She believes there is no one-size-fits-all loan, and she enjoys helping each client find the approach that fits their goals.

## The nurse's touch

Tracy brings the same care to her mortgage work that she brought to nursing. She is patient and honest, and she makes sure people understand each step. Because she is an investor herself, she understands cash flow, rehab budgets, timelines and long-term goals. She has sat on both sides of the table.

## Family first

Outside of work, Tracy's greatest joy is her family. She is the proud mother of two daughters and grandmother to six grandchildren, who keep her busy, laughing and grateful. Family is at the heart of everything she does.

Tracy and her husband also love to travel. They explore new places together whenever they can, and they come home with new stories, fresh perspective and a deeper appreciation for the people and places they love.
