FAQ

Common questions

What is a DSCR loan?

A Debt Service Coverage Ratio loan qualifies you based on the property's rental income compared to its monthly payment, rather than your personal income. It's popular with investors who are self-employed or own multiple properties.

Do I need experience to get a fix & flip loan?

Experience can improve your terms, but many lenders work with first-time flippers. We'll review your deal, budget and team to find a program that fits.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. Most investor programs, including DSCR and fix & flip loans, allow you to close in the name of your LLC or other business entity.

What's the benefit of using a mortgage broker?

A broker works with many lenders instead of one. That means more programs, more options and someone advocating for you to find terms that fit your goals.
