How fix & flip loans work

A fix and flip loan is short-term financing for buying a property that needs work, renovating it and selling or refinancing it. Lenders focus on the deal: the purchase price, your rehab budget, the after-repair value (ARV) and your experience.

Rehab funds are typically released in draws as work is completed and inspected. When the project is done, you sell, or you refinance into a long-term DSCR loan and keep it as a rental.

Fix & flip questions

Do fix and flip loans require tax returns?

No. Fix and flip loans qualify based on the deal, the after-repair value and your experience, so W-2s and tax returns are not required.

Can a first-time flipper get a fix and flip loan?

Yes. Many lenders work with first-time investors. Experience can improve leverage and pricing, and Tracy can help you present your deal, budget and team.

How are rehab funds paid out?

Rehab funds are usually held back and released in draws as each phase of work is completed and verified by inspection.

What happens when the rehab is done?

You can sell the property and repay the loan, or refinance into a long-term DSCR rental loan and keep it, which is the BRRRR strategy.
